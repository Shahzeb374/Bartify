01 / OUR STORY

An old idea.
A fresh beginning.

A book you’ve finished. A camera you rarely use. A skill someone else needs.

Useful things often sit unused while someone nearby is searching for them. We built Bartify to help bridge that gap—a place to discover what’s available and offer something of your own in return.

Created as a final-year project, Bartify explores how technology can make everyday exchange easier, more thoughtful, and more connected.
