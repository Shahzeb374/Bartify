A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Can I browse before signing up?

Yes. Explore available listings first. Sign in to list an item, send an offer, or use account features.

What should I include in a listing?

Clear photos, a useful description, the category and condition, and what you want in return. Include an estimated value if it helps explain your offer.

Does Bartify guarantee an exchange?

No. Members decide whether to accept an offer. Check the item and agree the details together; a moderation result is not a guarantee.
