THE PRIVACY BASICS
◎

What you share

Account details, profile photos, listings, offers, messages, and reports you submit through Bartify.

⇄

Why it is used

To support accounts, show listings, connect members, deliver messages, and review reports.

↗

Who can see it

Listing and displayed profile details are shared with visitors or members. Messages are intended for participants; reports go to administrators.

≡

Your choices

Choose what you post and use available editing controls. Clearing browser data may sign you out; it does not delete server records.

↻

A note on storage. Bartify stores login and profile data in your browser. External fonts and libraries receive connection information. Server retention and provider details are not yet confirmed for this FYP.
