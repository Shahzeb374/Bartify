Good exchanges

Six simple principles for being part of the Bartify community.

⇄
Explore Bartify ↗
01

Use your own account

Provide accurate details, keep your password private, and only use accounts you are authorised to access.

02

List honestly

Offer items you own or may exchange. Describe their condition accurately and use content you have permission to share.

03

Agree before you exchange

Confirm what is included, inspect the item, and agree collection or delivery. Estimated values are not guarantees.

04

Respect other members

No scams, harassment, spam, unlawful items, or misuse of personal information. Report suspicious activity.

05

Understand the platform

Bartify helps members connect; it cannot guarantee every listing or exchange. Moderation checks can be wrong.

06

Raise concerns responsibly

Discuss exchange problems with the other member and report abuse. Nothing here removes rights that applicable law protects.
